# Enterprise AI Pricing: Why Everything You Know Is Wrong

Nate argues that per-token and per-seat pricing are inherited metering schemes that leave the buyer carrying the burden of turning intelligence into ROI. Intelligence is a new economic input next to people and capital. Its value has to be discovered by the people using it, who become "managers of intelligence" whatever their title. He walks through Cedar's billing agent Cora (built on Sierra), where a call that explains a bill and takes payment is a closed-loop outcome that can be priced. A hypothetical equipment company shows how an engineer's domain judgment turns agent-assisted root-cause work into a new advisory product. He also covers forward-deployed engineering (OpenAI's deployment company, Anthropic's PE joint venture) and OpenAI's stake in Thrive Holdings, plus Shopify and Block as AI-fluent companies that can show ROI. He advises leaders to test their productivity claims with a concrete revenue project.

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/ai-roi-and-value-proposition]] — outcome pricing fits only closed-loop interactions; prove productivity with a previously unresourced revenue project and separate adoption from impact.
- [[concepts/ai-career-skills]] — the "manager of intelligence": domain judgment on evidence quality and when to stop the agent and bring in a human.
