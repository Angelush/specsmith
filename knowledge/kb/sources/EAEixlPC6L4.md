# Anthropic Just Filed Its S-1. The AI Market Is Way Smaller Than You Think. (For Now)

Nate reads Anthropic's S-1 from the customer side. More than half the Fortune 500 are substantial customers, yet the average public-company customer spends under $1 per $1,000 of total expenses, so he argues today's AI budgets understate the market. Most enterprises have deployed Claude to a few teams; Cowork (non-technical) is growing faster than Claude Code did, and more than half of $100K+ customers now use both the platform and a first-party app. Opus 5.5 cut cost per task about 40% versus Opus 5, but spend keeps rising (Jevons paradox). Workflow complexity and stickiness are the unmapped bottleneck. He answers his five June questions: gross margin rose from ~40% to ~85%, customers buy both software and deployment work, and it is unproven whether forward-deployed engineering is a bridge or a permanent requirement. Most of the video is market and valuation commentary. The builder-relevant points are measuring per useful task rather than per token, and designing each deployment so the next one is easier.
