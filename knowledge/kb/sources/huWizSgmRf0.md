# The 5 Skills I'd Bet On For 2027. AI Is Making Every One Worth More.

Nate argues the "AI is taking your skills" story is backwards: five skills have gained leverage as AI got more capable, namely taste, agency, focus, resourcefulness and follow-through. Each is tied to a concrete practice: build an alternative to the thing that keeps confusing people, let a rough first version reveal requirements you could not articulate, choose one problem and stay on it, learn at the moment of need on real projects, and carry work until the intended person actually benefits. He suggests revisiting projects previously ruled out by cost.
