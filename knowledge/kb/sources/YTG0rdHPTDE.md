# AI Is About To Spend Your Money. I Went To Stripe To Ask Who Stops It.

Nate interviews Emily, head of data/ML/agent infrastructure at Stripe, on what it takes to let agents transact. Key threads: AI abuse is pre-transaction (token theft via free trials, so fraud scoring moves from transactions to customers); agents need machine-readable pricing, usage-based billing and a spend-approval wallet; and outcome-based pricing is unsolved because buyers have no incentive to reveal how much they value an outcome, while relentless agent-to-agent negotiation could erode consumer surplus.

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/agentic-commerce]] — agents as buyers: machine-readable pricing (Machine Payments Protocol), usage-based billing for agents, wallet with approval thresholds, agent-to-agent negotiation eroding consumer surplus, and the "retractable leash" trust ramp.
- [[concepts/ai-security-and-trust]] — token theft is pre-transaction abuse; fraud scoring must shift from transactions to customers using cross-network signals.
- [[concepts/ai-roi-and-value-proposition]] — outcome-based pricing and the token-cost-plus-markup-with-evals model; why the SKU is dissolving.

## Notable quotes

- "Agents don't talk to sellers." (why product-led growth and usage-based pricing matter for agent customers)
- "Go on all the side quests you want, but before you spend my money, I need to approve."
