# GLM 5.2 Is Free And Beats Claude On Most Work. So Why Can't Companies Switch?

**Video:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zp8lr6IzUnQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zp8lr6IzUnQ) • **ID:** `Zp8lr6IzUnQ` • **Playlists:** P3

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/model-selection-frameworks]] — GLM 5.2 is the best model for "center of distribution" work (common patterns, easily inspected output); the hard, unanswered question is measuring whether your task load is center- or edge-of-distribution weighted before routing to a cheap open model.
- [[concepts/agent-harness-and-maintenance]] — switching models isn't a model call swap, it's replacing a whole work system (harness); the scarce "last-mile" talent to build harnesses—not raw intelligence—is why frontier providers keep pricing power and why team harnesses like Claude Tag risk "renting your company brain back to yourself."

## Notable quotes / hooks

- "You're not replacing a model call. You're actually replacing a whole work system."
- "A model can be an incredible brain in a jar. And it just isn't useful to you without a harness."
- "The firm has never faced a moment where the firm's brain has been on rent."
- "It's not a story of intelligence. It's a story of the last-mile in AI."
