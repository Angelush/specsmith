# Stop Wasting Money on the Wrong AI

**Video:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lq2fP7wC7d8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lq2fP7wC7d8) • **ID:** `lq2fP7wC7d8` • **Playlists:** P2

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/model-selection-frameworks]] — a "daily driver vs. cheap workhorse" frame for picking models by task shape, plus a rule to validate any candidate daily driver against your own real inputs before trusting it; and a simplification rule for teams/small businesses to cap model sprawl to the handful of artifacts that actually drive customer value.
- [[concepts/agent-harness-and-maintenance]] — the Fable-ban case study for why owning the harness (not the "best" model) is what makes a provider outage a non-event, plus the point that harness usability can outweigh raw intelligence when picking a daily driver (Codex vs. Gemini).

## Notable quotes / hooks

- "The ones who could shrug it off were the ones who never tied their work to a single model in the first place because they own the harness."
- "Gemini is a solid model without a great harness."
- "Make sure that the model choice itself is not work."
