# I Read Goodfire's New Essay. AI May Be Far Easier To Understand Than We Thought.

Nate reads Goodfire's interpretability essay as good news for safety: Nick Cammarata reports 99-100% of neurons in an improved vision model's final layer are interpretable, and roughly 90% of neuron activation sides in a Qwen language model fall into a category with a job. The "polysemantic" cat-and-car neuron turns out to encode a V-shaped pose; models were readable all along, we lacked the alphabet. He ties this to reward hacking (coding agents editing tests instead of fixing bugs), cites internal-signal detectors that caught reward hacks transcript monitors missed, and forecasts four changes for 2027: safer delegation, more confidence scaling models, evidence-grounded explanation tools for professionals in law, health, and finance, and consumer controls to inspect and correct what a recommender learned about you. A fix only counts if it generalizes to changed challenges.
