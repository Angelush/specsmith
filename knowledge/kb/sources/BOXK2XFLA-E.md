# Don't build more AI agents until you watch this

**Video:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BOXK2XFLA-E](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BOXK2XFLA-E) • **ID:** `BOXK2XFLA-E` • **Playlists:** P0

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/agent-harness-and-maintenance]] — Vercel made its agent better by deleting 80% of its tools; the harness (workbench) around an agent must be maintained and pruned because the model underneath keeps improving and the world around it drifts.

## Notable quotes / hooks

- "The beginner instinct is to add. The maintenance instinct is to ask what should be removed."
- "Agents can also break when the model gets better, and that is a different and new thing."
- "Agents are a lot less like apps and more like sailboats" (invoking Stewart Brand's *The Maintenance of Everything*).
- "A stale wiki that is annoying to you is incredibly dangerous to an agent."
