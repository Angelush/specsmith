# Your Roadmap Is Why You're Losing to AI-Native Teams.

**Video:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYcOFTMesGc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYcOFTMesGc) • **ID:** `hYcOFTMesGc` • **Playlists:** P3

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/organizational-ai-transformation]] — Roadmaps and product-directs-engineering handoffs are coordination overhead that cheap execution has made obsolete; product's job moves to daily terminal presence and jamming with engineering, and the 15 "commandments" only work as one interconnected system — partial adoption produces chaos, and the high-velocity culture at Anthropic/OpenAI is hired, taught, and reinforced as a whole, not a technique picked up piecemeal.
- [[concepts/ai-roi-and-value-proposition]] — The digital-photography analogy: cheap film once forced choices about which shots mattered; cheap AI execution removed that discipline without removing the importance of choosing, so an AI-heavy org must explicitly answer "what can we make now?" and "what are we unwilling to spend time on?" or volume swamps judgment.
- [[concepts/semantic-engineering]] — Documentation becomes literal executable infrastructure once agents read it to decide what to do (standard, source hierarchy, permissions, escalation path, definition of done); "AI slop" comes from the writer's missing clarity of intent, not from the AI, and there's no substitute for rigorous human writing discipline.
- [[concepts/ai-builder-mindset]] — Cheap individual output is not the same as product-market fit; taste, domain knowledge, customer connection, brand, and the courage to say "this doesn't work" still require a team, which is also what catches mistakes and clarifies intent for the AI.

## Notable quotes / hooks

- "If every decision has to be reexplained by a person, the humans become the rate limit on the business."
- "The cost of another draft, another prototype, another analysis, another piece of code is collapsing to zero... the choices did not become less important. They actually just became easier to avoid."
- "A designer who only designs screens is now designing the lobby while the rest of the building gets built without them."
