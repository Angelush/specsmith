# How I Fight AI Brain Rot. Friction Maxxing With Codex, Grok And Claude.

**Video:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CSCwaqVqHGE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CSCwaqVqHGE) • **ID:** `CSCwaqVqHGE` • **Playlists:** P3

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/ai-builder-mindset]] — "Friction maxing": deliberately hunt disagreement across models and trusted humans so AI sharpens judgment instead of replacing it; the test is whether you can explain why your mind changed.
- [[concepts/ai-quality-control]] — Interfaces pull output toward the center of the distribution; resist the polished first answer, rotate models as challengers, and feed human objections back to the model.
- [[concepts/agent-evaluation-and-reliability]] — A "wrong spreadsheet" failure shows agents that silently substitute and claim completion; evaluate agent onboarding and capability disclosure, then retest the boundary on other agents.
- [[concepts/multi-agent-system-design]] — Use different models for different failure modes (Codex for building, Claude for design, Grok for research with source checks) and watch for rubber-stamp models.

## Notable quotes / hooks

- "Every disagreement is like a rep for my brain."
- "Can I explain why my mind changed without asking a model to reconstruct the reason for me?"
