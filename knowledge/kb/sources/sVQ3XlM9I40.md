# Google's Gemini Argon Is #1 On A Leaderboard. It Hasn't Passed The Benchmark That Matters.

Nate argues leaderboards no longer tell you whether a model is useful: Google's Argon tops one but is barely accessible and has no clear customer surface. The better signal is customer obsession: teams that obsessively use their own product, notice what breaks and fix it. He illustrates with a multi-tool subscription audit (one tool consolidating connectors into a spreadsheet, another executing cancellations on the web) and a list of 26 agent use cases in three months, and concludes that the bottleneck has moved from models and benchmarks to product experience.

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/model-comparison-and-performance]] — judge a model by the job it does in a real workflow and by tool-specific strengths (connector depth vs speed), not leaderboard rank.
- [[concepts/ai-builder-mindset]] — dogfood the product and push the agent on real jobs; the bottleneck is now product experience.

## Notable quotes

- "The next year in AI is going to be a year of product, not a year of benchmarks."
- "The imperfection in our AI experiences... is not coming from the models. It's coming from the product experience."
