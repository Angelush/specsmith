# I Gave An AI Agent My Support Inbox. It Cut The Work By Two-Thirds.

Nate walks through how his company cut weekly support cases from 52 to 19 by using AI on the whole support process rather than the reply. Slack-access failures were the biggest pile; AI helped analyze, root-cause, fix (self-service approved domains, a non-expiring invite) and roll out. The playbook: write down and time the real process, give every pain its own ticket with pre-attached research, group cases by root cause, run drafts with human review, define end-to-end proof of resolution, and keep a weekly scorecard. Gumroad's support agent that fixed a bug through code and PR, then reopened when the customer rejected the result, is the more autonomous example.

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/practical-agent-adoption]] — automate the hidden work around the ticket and the root cause, pick a boring reversible first problem.
- [[concepts/knowledge-work-delegation]] — tickets as research scaffolds; keep human approval on access and money.
- [[concepts/agent-evaluation-and-reliability]] — end-to-end proof of done, surface system disagreement, draft-mode review, weekly scorecard.
