# I Stopped Installing Claude Skills. Here's What I Do Instead.

**Video:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=up0Bsf3f0Xc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=up0Bsf3f0Xc) • **ID:** `up0Bsf3f0Xc` • **Playlists:** P0, P2

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/prompting-and-skill-design]] — the core reframe that a skill is written for two audiences at once (the agent that loads/uses it, the human who must be able to read and audit it), plus the mechanics of progressive-disclosure loading and why "install and forget" fails 80% of the time compared to forking a trusted skill.
- [[concepts/agent-harness-and-maintenance]] — as skill libraries grow (10, 25, 50+ skills), unresolved conflicts between them get silently averaged out into duller results, and skills carry none of the trust guarantees of an app-store install, so the human must actively own and audit the set.

## Notable quotes / hooks

- "An agent skill is like a note that you leave for a worker who may not ask you a follow-up question before they just go out and get it done."
- "Skills are not apps... there's no app certificate. You have to sign it... it's not how it works with skills."
- "If you are not thoughtful about the conflicts between all the skills that you have as an advanced builder, you are going to have dull results because the AI is averaging out all of those conflicts."
