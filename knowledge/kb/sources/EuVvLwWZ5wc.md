# How to Use AI on Files You're Not Allowed to Upload

**Video:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EuVvLwWZ5wc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EuVvLwWZ5wc) • **ID:** `EuVvLwWZ5wc` • **Playlists:** P0

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/ai-security-and-trust]] — Demos "Airlock," a tool that strips PII/confidential material from a document before it reaches a frontier model: redaction should be scoped to the job (not blanket), rebuilt as a new file (not redacted in place) to avoid leaking hidden Office metadata, driven by a user-declared "protected terms" list that defaults uncertain matches to hidden, and skipped entirely when the sensitive content *is* the task. Cites Verizon telemetry on rising shadow-IT AI usage and NIST's "security fatigue" as the reason blanket "don't paste sensitive info" policies fail.

## Notable quotes / hooks

- "I don't want privacy to become a second project that cancels out the time AI was supposed to save."
- "Redaction is easy if you don't care whether the model can still help you. It's real hard if you care whether the model can still help you."
- "You should not have to become a privacy engineer before lunch to get your work done."
