# If OpenAI And Anthropic Are Discouraging You, You're Probably A Level 1 Builder.

Nate offers a five-level ladder of AI builders to explain why lab launches discourage some builders and not others. L1 loves the idea, L2 listens to customers and adapts, L3 owns go-to-market and uses AI to supercharge it, L4 has a deep problem-space thesis and an AI-based thesis that does not change with each news drop (example: voice as the next computing paradigm), and L5 forecasts what AI capabilities will unlock in their domain in 6 to 12 months and builds for them first. Domain detail is the moat labs cannot match. He gives the single key move for each level jump.
