# Salesforce Just Helped Microsoft. You're Picking The Winner Of A Trillion-Dollar Fight.

On October 27 Salesforce made Copilot Studio agents work with Salesforce, and Oracle announced a workspace that brings its apps into ChatGPT. Nate reads these rival-helps-rival deals as a fight over the "agentic control plane": the software that decides which information an agent can use, which instructions are current, who approves its actions, and where the result goes. Most of the video is market strategy. The parts useful to builders: connecting systems gives an agent data but not meaning; the hard-to-replace asset is the accumulated instructions, corrections, and skills, not the raw data; and buyers should write interoperability and swap-ability into requirements before choosing a control plane.

## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/agent-orchestration-architecture]] -- the control plane answers four questions for every agent job: allowed information, current instructions, approvers, and where the result goes.
- [[concepts/semantic-engineering]] -- connected data is not shared meaning; the business still has to supply definitions and judgment.
- [[concepts/open-brain-systems]] -- the real switching cost is accumulated instructions, corrections, and skills, and corrections do not carry forward on their own.
- [[concepts/enterprise-ai-adoption]] -- questions to ask before choosing (or building) a control plane, with interoperability written into purchase requirements.

## Notable quotes

- "Connecting the systems gets you access to that data, but someone is still going to have to establish what the information means for a decision in front of you."
- "Last month's correction does not automatically mean that it's going to make it into this month's hiring brief."
- "It's not that they want to own every agent, it's that they want to own the control plane."
