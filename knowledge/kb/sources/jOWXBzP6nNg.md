## Concepts covered

- [[concepts/model-comparison-and-performance]] — model lineages behave like families with resemblance across generations; compare qualitatively, not just by benchmark score.
- [[concepts/model-selection-frameworks]] — diagnose your own best-work process first, then pick the model that accelerates that loop; default to whichever model makes your hardest work most comfortable.
- [[concepts/agent-orchestration-architecture]] — pick the architect/orchestrator model for its intent-decomposition skill, not its benchmark rank, even after a newer model ships.
- [[concepts/knowledge-work-delegation]] — coding harnesses are engineer-tuned for engineers; first-gen knowledge-work harnesses still guess at non-engineer needs and risk "dumbing down" the work.
